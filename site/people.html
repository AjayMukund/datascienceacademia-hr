How access works

  • Admins: anyone with the admin role in the student portal sees everything here.
  • Students & staff see only their own record. They're linked when they sign in with the email on their record.
  • Parents see their child when they sign in with the guardian email on the child's record.
  • Trainers also see the student batches you tick under "Trainer for".
  • Fingerprints are never uploaded; only check-in and check-out times are.

Send invites

Each person gets an email with a link to set a password for hr.datascienceacademia.in. People who already have a portal login can just sign in.
